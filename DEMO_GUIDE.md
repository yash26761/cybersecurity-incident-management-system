## 🚀 How to Use

1. Click **Demo Guide** in the navigation bar or access [login.php](login.php).
2. Click **"Use Demo User"** or **"Use Demo Admin"**.
3. The email and password will automatically populate in the login form.
4. Manually click the **"🔐 Login"** button to authenticate and access the corresponding dashboard.
